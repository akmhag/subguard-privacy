Privacy Policy for SubGuard

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

SubGuard ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline-first subscription and free-trial manager designed with your privacy in mind.

1. Data Collection & Storage

SubGuard does not require an account, does not connect to your bank accounts, and does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or financial data on external servers. All subscription details, reminders, and settings you enter are stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database.

2. Backups & Exports

If you choose to export or import a CSV backup file, that file is created and managed solely on your device or in the storage location you explicitly choose.

3. Biometric Authentication

If you enable the optional biometric app lock, authentication is handled locally by your device's operating system (Android BiometricPrompt / Face ID). SubGuard never accesses or stores your biometric data.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Pro upgrades (subguard_pro_lifetime) are processed securely by Google Play Billing (or Apple App Store). We do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Notifications

SubGuard schedules local on-device notifications to remind you before subscriptions renew or free trials expire. No external push notification servers are used.
